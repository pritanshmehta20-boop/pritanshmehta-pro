01 / CONTEXT

A visual business needs visual communication.

For an architecture and interior design practice, social media is a place to present visual work, communicate a point of view and maintain a recognisable presence. The content needs to feel considered while remaining clear and easy to engage with on a fast-moving feed.

My contribution focused on the day-to-day work that supports that presence: designing graphics, editing video, organising content in calendars and helping the brand communicate consistently across social platforms.

The scope also extended beyond publishing. Social media activity supported audience growth and engagement, and was used as a channel to reach potential talent online.

02 / OBJECTIVE

Build a repeatable content rhythm.

The work brought several connected responsibilities together. The goal was to support a coherent social presence through planned content, suitable creative formats and ongoing interaction.

  • 02

    Create graphics and edit videos suited to social media consumption.
  • 03
    Support audience growth
    Contribute to the brand's ongoing social presence and efforts to attract a relevant audience.
  • 04
    Encourage interaction and discovery
    Support engagement and use social platforms to connect with potential candidates.

03 / CREATIVE PRODUCTION

Turn ideas into social content.

Graphic design and video editing were central parts of my contribution. Each format serves a different role: graphics can communicate a focused idea quickly, while video can give a subject more movement and context.

Graphic design

I worked on visual assets for social media, with attention to presenting information in a clear, feed-friendly format that fits a design-oriented brand.

Video editing

I edited video content for social platforms, helping shape raw visual material into a more structured viewing experience suitable for short-form digital consumption.

Consistency across formats

Both formats sat within the wider content plan. The aim was not to create isolated posts, but to support a steady stream of content that audiences could recognise and understand.

Creative principle

Make the work easy to notice—and easy to understand.

Strong visual presentation matters, but it needs structure and context. The creative should help the audience quickly understand what they are seeing and why it is relevant.

04 / CONTENT PLANNING

Consistency starts before publishing.

Content calendars helped organise the publishing process by giving planned content a place in the wider schedule. Planning makes it easier to coordinate creative production and keep track of what is intended to go live.

  • 01
    Plan ahead
    Organise content ideas and scheduled posts in a calendar.
  • 02
    Coordinate formats
    Account for both graphic and video content in the publishing plan.
  • 03
    Keep execution visible
    Use the calendar as a working reference for content preparation and publishing.
  • 04
    Leave room to learn
    Use audience response as a consideration for future content planning.

A calendar creates structure; it does not guarantee performance. Its value is in making content activity more organised and easier to review.

05 / GROWTH & ENGAGEMENT

Publishing is only one part of social.

Social media marketing also involves how people respond to content and whether the account gives them reasons to continue interacting. My responsibilities included supporting social media growth and engagement alongside content production.

Audience growth

Growth is most useful when it brings the account closer to the people it needs to reach. Content planning and consistent creative output provide a foundation for building that audience over time.

Engagement

Engagement is a set of audience signals rather than a single outcome. Reactions, comments, shares and other interactions can help indicate which content prompts a response, although they do not independently prove business impact.

Learn from response

Audience behaviour can inform future content choices. A useful next step is to review platform insights regularly and compare content against a clearly defined objective.

Results note
This case study documents the areas of work and responsibilities involved. No specific follower growth, engagement lift, recruitment result or business outcome is claimed because verified figures have not been provided for publication.
